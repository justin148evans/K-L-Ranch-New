Family Raised

K & L Ranch is centered around family and the animals we care for every day. We raise chickens and provide farm-fresh eggs, with a hands-on approach from hatch to adulthood.

Our goal is simple: care for the flock well, keep things local, and share what our ranch produces with people in our community.

What Matters Here

Hands-On Care

Our birds are part of daily ranch life, and we stay involved through every stage of their growth.

Local & Fresh

We focus on farm-fresh eggs and chickens rather than large-scale commercial production.

Growing Flock

Availability changes as chicks grow, hens lay, and our flock develops throughout the year.

From Chicks to the Coop

Raising chickens starts well before they join the adult flock. We care for chicks through their earliest stages, giving them a safe place to grow before moving outside.
